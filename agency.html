01 — The Problem
Traditional production is
broken for most brands.
Cost
A standard product campaign in Beirut costs $3K–$15K. In Dubai, $10K–$50K. Most SMBs simply cannot afford it.
Speed
2–4 weeks from brief to deliverable. In a world of weekly content cycles, that is too slow to compete.
Flexibility
Pay per shoot, not per output. No scalability. A brand that needs 30 assets pays for 30 separate production days.
Access
Smaller brands and agencies are priced out entirely. They underinvest in visuals, underperform on social, and lose market share.
This is a structural gap — not a preference gap. The demand for premium visual content is there. The access is not.
